Arash Goligerdian

Math Fellow, Department of Mathematics
University of California Riverside · Skye Hall, 271A

My research lies at the intersection of scientific computing and artificial intelligence. I develop algorithms that combine data-driven methods with first principles, such as the conservation of mass, energy, and momentum, so that AI models can address complex challenges in science and engineering. A central theme of my work is physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), which build physical laws, expressed as ordinary and partial differential equations, directly into the model. The result is solutions that are both accurate and consistent with the laws of nature, with applications in computational modeling, biomedical sciences, and engineering.

I am currently a Math Fellow at UC Riverside working with Prof. Maziar Raissi. I received my PhD in Applied Mathematics from the University of Houston (2025), and was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Texas A&M University before joining UC Riverside.
